Who We Are

Operators first. Consultants second.

We've built mission-critical systems at the enterprise scale, shipped startups from zero, and operated inside Fortune 100 companies. Now we build for the businesses that don't get McKinsey access.

Spear Flow helps growing businesses scale without adding headcount to do it. We are an AI-native consulting firm that audits how a company actually runs, quantifies where revenue and capacity are being lost, and builds the automation and AI systems that close the gap.

Most companies are not constrained by demand so much as by the operation sitting underneath it, and that cost never appears as a line item on any statement. It shows up instead as the lead that went to a faster competitor, the client who was never called back, and the month that closed three weeks late.

The shape of that constraint is different in every business, which is why it so rarely gets fixed. In one company the loss sits at intake, where inbound never gets answered fast enough to convert. In another it sits in delivery, where the work was sold on a promise the operation cannot keep. In another it is a visibility problem, where the numbers arrive too late to act on and the owner ends up making capital decisions on instinct. What all of them have in common is that the process lives in someone's head rather than in a system, so it fails the moment that person is busy, out, or gone for good.

The technology to solve this arrived all at once and is now available to everyone, which means owning it confers no advantage on its own. Buying software takes an afternoon, while identifying which part of the operation is actually costing you money and building something around it that your team is still running a year later is the work that produces a return.

So we start with the diagnosis rather than the build. We quantify annually what each gap is costing, let that number determine the scope, and stay out of everything else, whether the constraint sits in the intake layer, in the follow-up nobody has the hours for, or in the reporting that finally makes the operation legible to the person running it. Most firms sell a product and then go looking for a place to install it, which is why so much of this technology ends up unused, and it is also why we will tell you when the math does not support the work.

Everything we build comes from our founding team, operators from Raytheon, Vanguard, New York Life, and Cisco. We are engineers, strategists, and people who have run the operations they now automate.

FAQ

Questions we get asked most.

What does the system actually do?

Generates more revenue by automating lead generation, customer follow-ups, appointment booking, and customer lifecycle management. All in one place. Designed around how your business actually operates, not a generic template.

How does the AI follow up with leads?

The system responds to new leads through text, email, or chat within seconds. Sequences are trained on your tone and offers, so leads stay engaged without manual outreach from your team.

Will this work for a small team without dedicated sales staff?

That's exactly who this is built for. Owners, small operations, and growing teams without the budget to hire a full sales or customer service department.

What industries do you work with?

Real estate, healthcare clinics, home services, law firms, agencies, contractors, beauty businesses, and other service-based industries where lead response time directly drives revenue.

Will this replace my employees?

No. The goal is to free your team from repetitive work so they can focus on closing deals and serving customers. The system handles volume. Your team handles value.

How fast can we get started?

Most clients are fully deployed within a few weeks. Timeline depends on integrations, customization, and how clean your current data is. We tell you up front what's realistic.

Does it integrate with my current CRM?

In most cases, yes. The system is platform-adjacent and works with most major CRMs, calendars, communication tools, and lead sources. If we can't integrate cleanly, we'll tell you in the audit.

Can the AI schedule appointments automatically?

Yes. The system qualifies leads, suggests available times based on your calendar, and books appointments directly. No back-and-forth required.

What if a lead asks something the AI doesn't know?

We train and customize the system on your specific business so it handles common questions automatically. More complex or sensitive conversations get routed to your team in real time.

Is every setup custom?

Yes. Every implementation is built around your industry, team size, customer behavior, and goals. No off-the-shelf templates.

How does this actually increase revenue?

Faster response times convert more leads. Consistent follow-ups recover lost deals. Lifecycle automation increases repeat business. Most clients see measurable revenue impact within the first 90 days.

Do I need technical experience?

No. The system is built to be operator-friendly. Our team handles onboarding, setup, and training. You run your business. The system runs in the background.

Does it work after business hours?

24/7. Leads and customers get responses whether your office is open or not. Most of the revenue lift comes from after-hours coverage.

How much time does this save?

Most growing businesses save 10 to 20+ hours per week on repetitive communication and lead management. That's time you can spend selling, building, or going home.

Is customer data secure?

Yes. We follow industry best practices for data security, encryption in transit and at rest, and access controls. Specifics depend on your industry's compliance requirements, which we map during the audit.
