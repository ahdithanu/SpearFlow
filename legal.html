Legal

Every intake captured.
Your team billing,
not coordinating.

The Business Case

Law firms lose prospective clients at the first touchpoint — an unanswered call, a slow intake response, a consultation that never gets scheduled. Every missed intake is a case that went to a competitor who picked up.

Who We Work With

Solo practitioners, small firm partners, and practice managers at multi-attorney firms who need intake and client communication running on systems rather than depending on a single person to manage manually.

What changes
01

No intake goes unanswered.

Every inbound call handled 24/7. Prospective clients reach a responsive, professional first touchpoint at any hour. Consultation scheduling happens immediately, not the next business day.

02

Attorneys billing. Not scheduling.

Consultation confirmations, reminders, document request follow-ups, and status update communications run automatically. Billable time stays billable.

03

Full intake pipeline visibility.

See which practice area is converting consultations and which one's just burning intake calls — then put the intake hours where the cases actually are.

04

Client experience from first contact.

Clients form their opinion of your firm in the first interaction. A responsive, organized intake process signals the same professionalism as the work itself.

What we know
Intake

The intake call is the case.

Most prospective legal clients call two or three firms. The one that responds first with a clear, professional experience wins the engagement. A firm that calls back the next morning is competing against one that answered at 9pm. First response wins in legal intake the same way it does in real estate.

Billable Time

Admin work is the most expensive work attorneys do.

When a $400/hour attorney spends time on scheduling, follow-up emails, and status updates, those are $400/hour tasks by cost but zero by billing. Automating the administrative layer doesn't just save time. It recovers the highest-cost hours in the practice.

Client Trust

Operational professionalism signals legal professionalism.

Clients hire attorneys they trust. Trust starts forming before the first consultation. A firm that responds quickly, confirms appointments, and communicates proactively about document needs signals the same attention to detail clients expect in their matter. The administrative experience is part of the legal experience.
