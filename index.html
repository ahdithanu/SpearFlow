FAQs

Questions operators ask before they change a revenue system.

Does Spear Flow replace our CRM?

No. The goal is usually to make the systems you already have behave like one operation. We connect the workflow around the CRM rather than forcing a replacement when one is not needed.

What can Spear Flow connect to?

Typical deployments span forms, phone systems, CRM, email, SMS, calendars, advertising sources, and internal workflow tools. The exact architecture depends on the systems already in place.

Where does the AI actually sit?

Between customer signals and the next operational action. It can help interpret intent, qualify, route, draft, follow up, summarize context, or trigger the next workflow step depending on the use case.

Do humans stay in the loop?

Yes where the workflow requires it. We separate what can be automated confidently from what should require review, approval, or direct human judgment.

Is this a one time automation project?

The system is designed to evolve with the business. The initial deployment establishes the architecture, then workflows can be tuned as volume, channels, offers, and operational requirements change.

How do we know what to automate first?

We start with the handoffs that create measurable revenue loss: slow response, missed follow up, weak qualification, fragmented customer context, booking friction, and poor visibility into what happened after a lead arrived.
