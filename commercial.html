The Business Case

Commercial work runs on bid volume, GC relationships, and the permit-to-project pipeline, not on missed homeowner calls. The revenue leaks are in bid follow-up, invitation response speed, and staying top of GC lists, and most AI vendors have no idea how any of it works.

Who We Work With

Electrical, mechanical, plumbing, and specialty subcontractors bidding commercial work, plus service-side commercial contractors who live and die by GC relationships, bid turnaround, and the plan-review timeline.

What changes
01

Bid invitations never missed.

A GC's invitation to bid has a clock on it. Miss the window and you're off the job before you quoted. Intake captures and routes invitations the moment they land, so you're responding while your competitors are still checking email.

02

Bid follow-up that runs itself.

Submitting the bid is half the job. The award often goes to the sub who followed up, clarified scope, and stayed reachable. Structured follow-up keeps you in the GC's mind through the award decision instead of waiting silently.

03

GC relationships kept warm.

Your pipeline is a short list of GCs who invite you back. Systematic touchpoints keep you top of that list so the invitations keep coming, instead of hoping they remember you next cycle.

04

Project timeline tracked to permit.

Commercial jobs move on the plan-review and permit clock. We build visibility into where each project sits so your scheduling and crew planning track the approval timeline instead of guessing at it.

Invitations have a clock, and the clock wins jobs.

A bid invitation isn't a lead you can nurture at your pace. It has a submission deadline, and slow response reads as an unreliable sub. Being first and organized on the invitation is a competitive edge before the numbers are even in.

Commercial subs don't win on volume of inbound, they win on being on the invitation list of the GCs who feed them work. Staying top of mind with the right handful of relationships is worth more than any amount of cold marketing.
